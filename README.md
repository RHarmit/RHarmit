## What I Build

My projects sit at the intersection of **investing, risk management, market research, and data analytics**. I focus on tools that answer practical questions:

- Which securities have the strongest risk-adjusted setup?
- Where is momentum improving, and is volume confirming it?
- How should a portfolio be allocated across competing optimization frameworks?
- What happens to portfolio risk under different market conditions?
- How can fixed-income, structured-credit, and reconciliation workflows be automated?

Rather than treating finance and programming as separate skills, I use Python, SQL, APIs, and web applications to make financial analysis faster, more transparent, and easier to act on.
